Frequently Asked Questions

Use your roll number as the username. Your default password is emis@<roll_number> (for example emis@BIM-2083-001). Change your password after first login if required by contacting the administration office.

Visit Attendance from the top navigation. You can filter by month and see your attendance rate along with daily records.

Results appear under Results as soon as the examination office publishes them. Subject-wise marks are listed along with semester GPA and status.

The Fees page shows your fee structure, total paid, outstanding balance, and every payment receipt. For payment issues, contact the accounts office.

Books are issued by the librarian at the circulation desk. Simply visit the library with your student ID; the librarian will record the loan against your roll number. Overdue books incur a fine of Rs. 5 per day.
